# ISO/IEC 27001:2013 Annex A — Full Control Catalogue (114 Controls)

*Reconstructed reference — regenerated on 2026-09-11 to replace a file missing from the original skill upload; verify against the official ISO publications before relying on citations.*

The 2013 revision structures Annex A into **114 controls across 14 domains (A.5–A.18)**, each domain divided into control objectives. Note: all ISO 27001:2013 certificates expired by **October 2025** — this catalogue is retained for legacy gap analyses, transition assessments and historical audit context. For current work, default to the 2022 catalogue (`annex-a-2022.md`) and use `control-mapping.md` for cross-referencing.

## A.5 Information security policies

**Objective A.5.1 — Management direction for information security**: provide management direction and support for information security in line with business requirements, laws and regulations.

## A.7 Human resource security

**Objective A.7.1 — Prior to employment**: ensure employees and contractors understand their responsibilities and are suitable for their roles.
**Objective A.7.2 — During employment**: ensure employees and contractors are aware of and fulfil their security responsibilities.
**Objective A.7.3 — Termination and change of employment**: protect the organisation's interests as part of the process of changing or terminating employment.

## Usage Notes for This Skill

- Use this catalogue only when the user explicitly works with a legacy 2013 ISMS, historical audit records or contract references to 2013 control numbering.
- For any 2013 control the user cites, offer the 2022 successor via `control-mapping.md` — every 2013 control maps to at least one 2022 control (none were dropped outright; several were merged).
- 2013 SoAs list controls at the third level (e.g., A.9.2.3); 2022 SoAs list them at two levels (e.g., 8.2). Preserve the granularity the user's documentation uses.
