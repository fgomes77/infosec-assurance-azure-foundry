# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A — Full Control Catalogue (93 Controls)

*Reconstructed reference — regenerated on 2026-09-11 to replace a file missing from the original skill upload; verify against the official ISO publications before relying on citations.*

The 2022 revision restructures Annex A into **93 controls across 4 themes**, aligned with ISO/IEC 27002:2022. Each control also carries five attribute taxonomies (control type, information security properties, cybersecurity concepts, operational capabilities, security domains) — use these for filtering and SoA grouping.

| Theme | Controls | Range |
|-------|----------|-------|

| 6 People | 8 | 6.1–6.8 |

| 8 Technological | 34 | 8.1–8.34 |

Controls marked **[NEW]** are the 11 controls introduced in 2022 with no direct 2013 predecessor.

## Theme 5 — Organizational Controls (5.1–5.37)

**5.1 Policies for information security** — Define, approve, publish and communicate an information security policy and topic-specific policies; review at planned intervals and after significant change.

**5.2 Information security roles and responsibilities** — Define and allocate security roles and responsibilities according to organisational needs; document them (e.g., RACI) so accountability is clear.

**5.3 Segregation of duties** — Separate conflicting duties and areas of responsibility to reduce risk of misuse or unintentional modification of assets.

**5.4 Management responsibilities** — Management must require all personnel to apply security in accordance with established policies and procedures.

**5.5 Contact with authorities** — Establish and maintain contact channels with relevant authorities (regulators, law enforcement, CERTs) for incident and compliance purposes.

**5.6 Contact with special interest groups** — Maintain contact with security forums, professional associations and threat-sharing communities to stay current.

**5.7 Threat intelligence [NEW]** — Collect and analyse information about security threats to produce actionable intelligence at strategic, tactical and operational levels; feed it into risk assessment and controls.

**5.8 Information security in project management** — Integrate security into project management regardless of project type; assess and treat security risks in every project.

**5.9 Inventory of information and other associated assets** — Develop and maintain an inventory of information and associated assets, each with an assigned owner.

**5.10 Acceptable use of information and other associated assets** — Identify, document and implement rules for acceptable use and handling of information and assets (acceptable use policy).

**5.11 Return of assets** — Personnel and other interested parties must return organisational assets upon change or termination of employment/contract.

**5.12 Classification of information** — Classify information based on confidentiality, integrity, availability and interested-party requirements; keep the scheme proportionate.

**5.13 Labelling of information** — Develop and implement procedures for labelling information in line with the classification scheme, covering physical and electronic formats.

**5.14 Information transfer** — Establish rules, procedures and agreements for transferring information within the organisation and with external parties (electronic, physical, verbal).

**5.15 Access control** — Establish and implement rules for physical and logical access to information and assets based on business and security requirements.

**5.16 Identity management** — Manage the full lifecycle of identities (creation, modification, removal); ensure unique identities for accountability.

**5.17 Authentication information** — Control allocation and management of authentication information (passwords, keys, tokens) and advise users on proper handling.

**5.18 Access rights** — Provision, review, modify and revoke access rights in line with the access control policy; recertify periodically.

**5.19 Information security in supplier relationships** — Define and implement processes to manage security risks associated with supplier products and services.

**5.20 Addressing information security within supplier agreements** — Establish and agree relevant security requirements with each supplier based on the relationship type.

**5.21 Managing information security in the ICT supply chain** — Manage risks associated with the ICT product and service supply chain, including sub-suppliers and component provenance.

**5.22 Monitoring, review and change management of supplier services** — Regularly monitor, review, evaluate and manage changes in supplier security practices and service delivery.

**5.23 Information security for use of cloud services [NEW]** — Establish processes for acquiring, using, managing and exiting cloud services in line with security requirements (shared responsibility, exit strategy).

**5.24 Information security incident management planning and preparation** — Plan and prepare for incident management: define processes, roles and responsibilities before incidents occur.

**5.25 Assessment and decision on information security events** — Assess security events and decide whether they are to be categorised as security incidents.

**5.26 Response to information security incidents** — Respond to incidents in accordance with documented procedures (containment, escalation, communication).

**5.27 Learning from information security incidents** — Use knowledge gained from incidents to strengthen and improve controls (post-incident review, lessons learned).

**5.28 Collection of evidence** — Establish procedures for identification, collection, acquisition and preservation of evidence related to security events (forensic readiness, chain of custody).

**5.29 Information security during disruption** — Plan how to maintain information security at an appropriate level during disruption (linked to business continuity).

**5.30 ICT readiness for business continuity [NEW]** — Plan, implement, maintain and test ICT readiness based on business continuity objectives and ICT continuity requirements (RTO/RPO, failover tests).

**5.31 Legal, statutory, regulatory and contractual requirements** — Identify, document and keep up to date the legal/regulatory/contractual requirements relevant to information security, and the approach to meeting them.

**5.32 Intellectual property rights** — Implement procedures to protect intellectual property rights (licensing compliance, proprietary information).

**5.33 Protection of records** — Protect records from loss, destruction, falsification, unauthorised access and release, per legal and business requirements (retention schedules).

**5.34 Privacy and protection of PII** — Identify and meet requirements for preservation of privacy and protection of PII per applicable laws and contracts (e.g., GDPR alignment).

**5.35 Independent review of information security** — Review the organisation's approach to managing information security independently at planned intervals or when significant changes occur.

**5.36 Compliance with policies, rules and standards for information security** — Regularly review compliance with the security policy, topic-specific policies and standards (manager reviews, technical compliance checks).

**5.37 Documented operating procedures** — Document operating procedures for information processing facilities and make them available to personnel who need them.

## Theme 6 — People Controls (6.1–6.8)

**6.1 Screening** — Perform background verification checks on candidates prior to and, where warranted, during employment, proportionate to business requirements and risk.

**6.2 Terms and conditions of employment** — Employment contracts must state personnel and organisational responsibilities for information security.

**6.3 Information security awareness, education and training** — Provide appropriate awareness, education, training and regular policy updates relevant to each job function.

**6.4 Disciplinary process** — Formalise and communicate a disciplinary process for personnel who commit an information security policy violation.

**6.5 Responsibilities after termination or change of employment** — Define, enforce and communicate security responsibilities that remain valid after termination or change of employment (NDAs, ongoing confidentiality).

**6.6 Confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements** — Identify, document, review and sign confidentiality/NDA agreements reflecting the organisation's information protection needs.

**6.7 Remote working** — Implement security measures when personnel work remotely to protect information accessed, processed or stored outside premises.

**6.8 Information security event reporting** — Provide a mechanism for personnel to report observed or suspected security events through appropriate channels in a timely manner.

## Theme 7 — Physical Controls (7.1–7.14)

**7.1 Physical security perimeters** — Define and use security perimeters to protect areas containing information and associated assets.

**7.2 Physical entry** — Protect secure areas with appropriate entry controls and access points (badges, visitor management, reception).

**7.3 Securing offices, rooms and facilities** — Design and implement physical security for offices, rooms and facilities.

**7.4 Physical security monitoring [NEW]** — Continuously monitor premises for unauthorised physical access (CCTV, alarms, guards); protect monitoring records and test detection mechanisms.

**7.5 Protecting against physical and environmental threats** — Design and implement protection against physical and environmental threats (natural disaster, malicious attack, accidents).

**7.6 Working in secure areas** — Design and implement security measures for working in secure areas (supervision, restrictions on recording devices).

**7.7 Clear desk and clear screen** — Define and enforce clear desk rules for papers/removable media and clear screen rules for information processing facilities.

**7.8 Equipment siting and protection** — Site equipment securely and protect it from environmental threats and unauthorised access.

**7.9 Security of assets off-premises** — Protect off-site assets (laptops, mobile devices, removable media) taking account of the different risks outside premises.

**7.10 Storage media** — Manage storage media through their lifecycle: acquisition, use, transportation and disposal, per classification and handling requirements.

**7.11 Supporting utilities** — Protect information processing facilities from power failures and other disruptions caused by supporting utility failures (UPS, generators, HVAC).

**7.12 Cabling security** — Protect cables carrying power or data from interception, interference or damage.

**7.13 Equipment maintenance** — Maintain equipment correctly to ensure availability, integrity and confidentiality of information.

**7.14 Secure disposal or re-use of equipment** — Verify that sensitive data and licensed software are removed or securely overwritten before disposal or re-use of equipment.

## Theme 8 — Technological Controls (8.1–8.34)

**8.1 User endpoint devices** — Protect information stored on, processed by or accessible via user endpoint devices (device hardening, MDM, encryption).

**8.2 Privileged access rights** — Restrict and manage the allocation and use of privileged access rights (just-in-time, separate admin accounts, review).

**8.3 Information access restriction** — Restrict access to information and assets in accordance with the topic-specific access control policy (need-to-know).

**8.4 Access to source code** — Appropriately manage read and write access to source code, development tools and software libraries.

**8.5 Secure authentication** — Implement secure authentication technologies and procedures based on access restrictions and policy (MFA, secure log-on).

**8.6 Capacity management** — Monitor and adjust resource use in line with current and expected capacity requirements.

**8.7 Protection against malware** — Implement malware protection supported by appropriate user awareness.

**8.8 Management of technical vulnerabilities** — Obtain information about technical vulnerabilities, evaluate exposure and take appropriate measures (scanning, patch management, remediation SLAs).

**8.9 Configuration management [NEW]** — Establish, document, implement, monitor and review configurations (including security configurations) of hardware, software, services and networks; use baselines and detect drift.

**8.10 Information deletion [NEW]** — Delete information stored in systems, devices or other media when no longer required (retention alignment, secure deletion methods, cloud deletion verification).

**8.11 Data masking [NEW]** — Use data masking, pseudonymisation or anonymisation in accordance with policy and business/legal requirements, especially for PII in non-production environments.

**8.12 Data leakage prevention [NEW]** — Apply data leakage prevention measures to systems, networks and devices that process, store or transmit sensitive information (DLP tooling, egress monitoring).

**8.13 Information backup** — Maintain and regularly test backup copies of information, software and systems per the topic-specific backup policy.

**8.14 Redundancy of information processing facilities** — Implement sufficient redundancy in information processing facilities to meet availability requirements.

**8.15 Logging** — Produce, store, protect and analyse logs recording activities, exceptions, faults and other relevant events.

**8.16 Monitoring activities [NEW]** — Monitor networks, systems and applications for anomalous behaviour and take appropriate actions to evaluate potential security incidents (SIEM, baselining, alerting).

**8.17 Clock synchronization** — Synchronise clocks of information processing systems to approved time sources for reliable log correlation.

**8.18 Use of privileged utility programs** — Restrict and tightly control use of utility programs capable of overriding system and application controls.

**8.19 Installation of software on operational systems** — Implement procedures and measures to securely manage software installation on operational systems (change control, allowlisting).

**8.20 Networks security** — Secure, manage and control networks and network devices to protect information in systems and applications.

**8.21 Security of network services** — Identify, implement and monitor security mechanisms, service levels and requirements of network services.

**8.22 Segregation of networks** — Segregate groups of information services, users and systems in networks (zoning, VLANs, micro-segmentation).

**8.23 Web filtering [NEW]** — Manage access to external websites to reduce exposure to malicious content (URL filtering, block categories, exception handling).

**8.24 Use of cryptography** — Define and implement rules for effective use of cryptography, including key management, throughout the information lifecycle.

**8.25 Secure development life cycle** — Establish and apply rules for secure development of software and systems (security gates across SDLC).

**8.26 Application security requirements** — Identify, specify and approve information security requirements when developing or acquiring applications.

**8.27 Secure system architecture and engineering principles** — Establish, document, maintain and apply secure engineering principles to any system development activity (defence in depth, secure defaults, zero trust concepts).

**8.28 Secure coding [NEW]** — Apply secure coding principles to software development (coding standards, code review, dependency management, developer tooling and training).

**8.29 Security testing in development and acceptance** — Define and implement security testing processes in the development lifecycle (SAST/DAST, penetration testing, acceptance criteria).

**8.30 Outsourced development** — Direct, monitor and review activities related to outsourced system development (requirements in contracts, code delivery review).

**8.31 Separation of development, test and production environments** — Separate and secure development, testing and production environments to reduce risk of unauthorised change or data exposure.

**8.32 Change management** — Subject changes to information processing facilities and systems to change management procedures.

**8.33 Test information** — Appropriately select, protect and manage information used for testing (avoid or mask production data in test).

**8.34 Protection of information systems during audit testing** — Plan and agree audit tests and other assurance activities involving operational systems between the tester and appropriate management to minimise operational impact.

## Usage Notes for This Skill

- **SoA generation**: list all 93 controls; mark applicability, justification and implementation status per control.
- **Gap analysis**: group findings by theme; the Organizational theme carries most governance-heavy controls, Technological carries most engineering evidence.
- **Attributes**: 27002:2022 assigns each control attributes (#Preventive/#Detective/#Corrective; #Confidentiality/#Integrity/#Availability; #Govern/#Protect/#Detect/#Respond/#Recover; operational capabilities; security domains). Cite them when a user wants NIST CSF-style views.
