# cyber-forum — Foundry overlay

(Appended after the SKILL.md body; environment mapping only.)

- Inline answers stay inline. A "structured written brief" is emitted as
  the `ThreatIntelBrief` DOCX contract and stored by the
  `cyber-forum-brief` pipeline (`Reports/<Supplier>/<Service>/` or
  `Advisory/<Topic>/<Subtopic>/` for non-supplier topics) — never
  `/mnt/user-data/outputs/` + `present_files`.
- Research method: apply `overlays/research-pattern.md` (source tiers,
  citation discipline, gaps section) and the egress rule.
- Literature-style questions spanning many sources → hand off to
  `research_coordinator` when attached.
